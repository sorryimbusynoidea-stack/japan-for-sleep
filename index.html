Japan as a way of seeing life.

JAPAN FOR SLEEP is not only about places, dates, or historical events.

It is about the ideas behind them — silence, impermanence, attention, simplicity, craftsmanship, nature, ritual, and the ways people have learned to live with time.

Every story begins with Japan. But sometimes, somewhere along the way, it becomes a story about how we see our own lives.
